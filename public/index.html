Questions shop owners ask.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Keep your number and forward calls to your AI number when you're busy or closed. Most carriers call this conditional call forwarding.

Will callers know it's AI?

It introduces itself as your virtual assistant, tells callers the call is recorded, and answers honestly if asked. Emergencies and urgent no-heat calls can be connected straight to your on-call tech. Anyone else who wants a person gets a callback.

Where do the bookings go?

Each booking and message is emailed to you right after the call, with the caller's name, number, address, problem and urgency.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see a summary of every call. If it isn't sure, it takes a message instead of guessing, and you can change what it says at any time.
