Questions

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls when you're busy or closed.

Will callers know it's AI?

It introduces itself as your virtual assistant and answers honestly if asked. Anyone who wants a person gets transferred or a callback.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see every call summary, and we review calls with you each month. If it isn't sure, it takes a message instead of guessing.
