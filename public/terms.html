The service

AfterHours Desk provides an AI phone receptionist for your business. It answers calls you forward to it, books appointments into the windows you set, takes messages, and sends you summaries by email and, if you choose, by text message.

Free month and payment

Your first month is free and starts when your receptionist goes live. After that the plan price shown at sign-up applies, billed monthly in advance through our payment provider. If no payment method is added after the free month and a short grace period, we may pause your receptionist; calls then go to your own fallback number. You can cancel any month. Fees already paid are not refunded unless the law requires it.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate business details, hours, fees and emergency numbers, and keep them up to date in your dashboard.
  • Tell callers, where the law requires, that calls may be recorded and answered by an AI system. Our greeting includes this notice; you are responsible for any additional notice your location requires.
  • Only send text messages to people who gave you their number for that purpose, and follow the texting rules where you operate.
  • Do not use the service for anything unlawful, or for emergency dispatch.

Not an emergency service

The receptionist gives general safety instructions, such as telling a caller to leave a building and call the emergency number. It is not a substitute for emergency services, and it can make mistakes. You remain responsible for responding to your customers.

AI limitations

Bookings and messages are taken by an AI system and may sometimes be incomplete or wrong. Check important details with the customer. We do not guarantee that every call will be answered, for example during outages of telephone or AI providers.

Your data

You own your business data and your customers' details. We process them to provide the service, as described in our privacy policy. You can ask us to close your account and delete your data at any time from your dashboard.
